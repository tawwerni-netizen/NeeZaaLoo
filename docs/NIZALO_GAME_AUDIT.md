## Deep Dive: Game-by-Game Audit

### 01. Chess (`chess`)
- **First Impression:** Grandmaster prestige. High-end dark wood and emerald themes.
- **Controls & Gameplay:** Smooth drag-and-drop or click-to-move with optimistic UI updates. Legal move highlights and promotion selector modal.
- **Clock & HUD:** FIDE 3+2 Blitz clock or 60s per move. Responsive audio on moves, captures, check, and checkmate.
- **Mobile UX:** Board scales down nicely to 360px viewport. Promotion dialog is properly centered.
- **Fair Play:** 100% server-authoritative move validation via `chess.js` engine in backend. Anti-cheat move-timing monitoring.
- **V2 Optimization:** Ensure touch gestures do not trigger page scroll on mobile Safari/Chrome. Provide 1-click "Practice vs Computer" directly from hero.

---

### 02. Dominoes (`dominoes`)
- **First Impression:** Authentic Middle Eastern and Latin American café culture. 28 double-six domino tiles.
- **Controls & Gameplay:** Click/tap tile to place on left or right open table head. Draw from boneyard when blocked.
- **Visuals & Feedback:** Heavy bone-thud sound effect on tile placement. Clear pip indicators.
- **Mobile UX:** Horizontal table layout requires horizontal pan or smart auto-centering of the domino snake.
- **Fair Play:** Tile distribution generated by server CSPRNG. Opponent's hand is never sent to the client.
- **V2 Optimization:** Add subtle auto-zoom on mobile when the tile chain extends past 6 tiles to prevent overflow.

---

### 03. Ludo (`ludo`)
- **First Impression:** Highly vibrant, colorful 15x15 board with 3D cyber-die and glossy pawns. Supports 2 and 4 players.
- **Controls & Gameplay:** Tap die to roll, tap pawn to advance. Auto-skips when no legal moves exist.
- **Visuals & Feedback:** Animated 3D dice rolls with authentic tumbling physics audio. Star safe-zone indicators.
- **Mobile UX:** 15x15 grid requires precise touch targets for pawns sharing the same square.
- **Fair Play:** Dice roll computed on server before animation. Impossible to spoof rolls.
- **V2 Optimization:** Group overlapping pawns on safe star squares with an interactive fan-out tap selector to prevent misclicks.

---

### 04. Backgammon (`backgammon`)
- **First Impression:** Classic Mediterranean/Levantine heritage. 24 points, doubling cube, and bar.
- **Controls & Gameplay:** Alternating dice rolls, bearing off tray, doubling cube offers.
- **Visuals & Feedback:** Ivory dice sound, smooth checker slide sound.
- **Fair Play:** Server CSPRNG dice generation, strict legal move generator.
- **V2 Optimization:** Clarify doubling cube rules for beginner players with a simple 1-line tooltip explaining how doubling the stake works.

---

### 05. Speed Math (`speed-math`)
- **First Impression:** High adrenaline mental athletics. 60-second shared synchronous clock.
- **Controls & Gameplay:** Numeric keypad or keyboard input. Instant equation validation.
- **Visuals & Feedback:** Streak combo multiplier (x2, x3, x5) with fire animation effects and audio tones.
- **Mobile UX:** Integrated on-screen 0–9 keypad prevents mobile virtual keyboard from obscuring the equation.
- **Fair Play:** Exactly identical equations served to both players simultaneously from server seed.
- **V2 Optimization:** Emphasize Speed Math as the **Ultimate 60-Second Onboarding Game** for new visitors to test the platform in under 1 minute with 0 financial risk.

---

### 06. XO / Tic-Tac-Toe Blitz (`xo`)
- **First Impression:** Lightning speed. 3x3 grid, 5-second per move sudden death.
- **Controls & Gameplay:** Instant single tap to place X or O.
- **Policy Note:** Marked as Free Only due to solved game theory. Used for quick fun, reflexes, and friendly challenges.
- **V2 Optimization:** Perfect casual filler game. Highlight "Play in 30 Seconds" badge.

---

### 07. Connect Four (`connect-four`)
- **First Impression:** 7 columns, 6 rows vertical gravity drop.
- **Controls & Gameplay:** Tap column header to drop token into lowest available slot.
- **Visuals & Feedback:** Smooth vertical drop gravity animation and plastic clack sound.
- **V2 Optimization:** Free-to-play strategy duel. Ideal for quick tactical showdowns.

---

### 08. Checkers (`checkers`)
- **First Impression:** 8x8 American Checkers with diagonal moves and mandatory jumps.
- **Controls & Gameplay:** Click piece, click destination. Multi-jump sequencing with King promotion crown.
- **Fair Play:** Server-authoritative mandatory jump validation.
- **V2 Optimization:** Clear visual ghost trail showing multi-jump path options when multiple captures are available.

---

### 09. Reversi / Othello (`reversi`)
- **First Impression:** 8x8 green felt board. Black and white flipping discs.
- **Controls & Gameplay:** Tap legal flank square; trapped discs flip over smoothly.
- **Visuals & Feedback:** Smooth 3D disc flip animation and score counters showing territorial share.
- **V2 Optimization:** Real-time visual indicator of how many enemy discs will flip on hover/tap.

---

### 10. Gomoku (`gomoku`)
- **First Impression:** Traditional 15x15 goban board with black and white stones.
- **Controls & Gameplay:** Tap intersection to place stone. Win condition: unbroken 5-in-a-row.
- **V2 Optimization:** Clear highlight of open-three and open-four threats to help beginners avoid early traps.

---

### 11. Seega (`seega`)
- **First Impression:** Timeless Arab heritage mind sport. 5x5 board.
- **Controls & Gameplay:** Two distinct phases: Phase 1 (placing tokens alternatively), Phase 2 (sliding and pincer flanking captures).
- **V2 Optimization:** Educational mini-tutorial overlay for Phase 1 to Phase 2 transition so new players understand when placement ends and movement begins.
